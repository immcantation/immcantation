External Resources
===========================================================================================

Here we provide links to external resources that can help you get started 
with Immcantation and Adaptive Immune Receptor Repertoire (AIRR) research
in general. Whether you're new to immunogenomics or looking to deepen your 
understanding of the field, these resources will provide insights 
and guidance.

Applications of Immcantation
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Looking for inspiration on how to apply Immcantation to your research? 

+ The :ref:`citedby` page lists scientific publications that have used Immcantation
  in their work. Browse these papers to discover how researchers across different 
  fields have leveraged the framework for diverse applications, from vaccine 
  development to autoimmune disease studies.

+ Visit the |immcantation_youtube| to access tutorials, webinars, and presentations 
  on using Immcantation tools. Recordings of our yearly |immcantation_users| 
  are also available on the channel.

.. |immcantation_youtube| raw:: html

    <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@immcantation" target="_blank">Immcantation YouTube channel</a>

.. |immcantation_users| raw:: html

    <a href="https://immcantation.github.io/users-meeting/" target="_blank">Immcantation Users Group Meeting</a>

Learn about immune repertoires and AIRR-seq research
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

+ The |airr_c| of The Antibody Society is a research-driven group that is 
  organizing and coordinating stakeholders in the use of next-generation 
  sequencing technologies to study antibody/B-cell  and T-cell receptor 
  repertoires.  The Community also organizes meetings and workshops 
  to report progress in the field and promote its standards as well as 
  |airr_webinars| and |airr_seminars| to promote AIRR-seq related research.

.. |airr_c| raw:: html

    <a href="https://www.antibodysociety.org/the-airr-community/" target="_blank">Adaptive Immune Receptor Repertoire Community (AIRR-C)</a>
